The journal items created when an invoice is reconciled take the analytic
distribution of the invoice, so the profit and loss of a project or branch
includes them:

- the income or expense line of the **exchange difference** entry;
- the income or expense **write-off** of a payment registered with a
  difference ("Mark as fully paid").

The distribution is the one of the invoice lines, weighted by their amount.
When several invoices are reconciled together, the most recent one is used.
